([MIT Press](https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262581066/connectionist-symbol-processing/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Connectionist Symbol Processing"))

([Springer Nature](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-1-4757-3922-0_14?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Artificial Neural Networks and Genetic Algorithms"))

([Springer Nature](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-642-35289-8_32?utm_source=chatgpt.com "A Practical Guide to Training Restricted Boltzmann Machines"))
